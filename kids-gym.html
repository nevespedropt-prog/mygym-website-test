Kids Gym

Gym time just for kids, every Tuesday and Thursday from 4pm to 5pm.

A gym session made for children

Kids Gym is a one hour session just for children aged 8 and over. While it runs, the gym is closed to adult members, so the children have the space to themselves with their coach.

Every session is led by one coach, with up to 12 children.

For parents

  • Parents do not have to stay, but you remain responsible for your child during the session.
  • Children with special needs must have someone to supervise them alongside the coach.
  • A parent or guardian completes the health and safety form and accepts the terms and conditions on their child's behalf.

Safety

  • Our coaches are DBS checked.
  • We have public liability insurance, with £1 million of cover.

Booking

Book a place through the class timetable, or just turn up while there are places left.
